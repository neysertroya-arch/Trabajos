## AI policy

**PASS.**

ERAE's author instructions require transparent disclosure of AI use in the cover letter and Methods or Acknowledgements. OUP's July 2026 journal AI policy explicitly treats the following as typically permitted with disclosure:
- substantial editing/reorganizing of text;
- collecting/organizing/formatting/analyzing/visualizing data using AI;
- generating or refining code using AI.

The documented workflow for this manuscript (literature-search planning, manuscript organization/language editing, reproducibility-workflow planning/review, with all quantitative analysis executed and verified by authors in R) is compatible with this policy when disclosed accurately.

Do not minimize or conceal the documented AI use.
