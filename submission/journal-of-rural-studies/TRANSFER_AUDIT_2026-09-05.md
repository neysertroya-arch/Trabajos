# Journal of Rural Studies transfer audit — 2026-09-05

## Decision

**GO — transfer candidate**, subject to final author review and portal verification.

## Why the journal fits

The journal explicitly covers contemporary rural societies and economies, rural policy, agricultural and rural economics, planning, and interdisciplinary rural social science with global empirical scope. Recent articles include work on land regularization and agricultural technical efficiency in Andean countries, land certification and factor reallocation in rural China, and land tenure reform and smallholder livelihood resilience.

## Current submission requirements checked

- Research article: compatible.
- Normal maximum length: 10,000 words.
- Double-anonymized review: required.
- Editable Word source, single column: required.
- Abstract: maximum 250 words.
- Keywords: 1–7, English.
- Highlights: required, 3–5 bullets, maximum 85 characters each.
- Figures: separate artwork files with captions; vector PDF/EPS accepted.
- Research data: Option C — repository deposit, citation and linking required, or an explanation if sharing is not possible.
- Data availability statement: required at submission.
- Generative AI: disclosure required when applicable under Elsevier policy.
- Subscription route: no publishing charge according to the Elsevier transfer offer; optional OA carries an APC.

## Adaptation scope

Only journal-specific framing and editorial files are changed. The analytical sample, models, coefficients, p-values, tables, figures, and substantive inference remain unchanged.

## Transfer gate

Do not complete the transfer until:
1. the JRS-specific manuscript and package pass a focused editorial/reproducibility audit;
2. both authors approve the adapted version;
3. the transfer portal still shows subscription with no publishing charge;
4. any portal-specific declarations are completed.
